INICS · Where We Sense

Where Sensors Work

A joint's angle, a wheel's distance, the count on a line. Select a number to see what INICS reads at each site.

01
Robot Joint

One angle per joint, without wavering

In a six-axis robot every joint moves relative to the angles of the others. As joints get thinner, the encoder inside must be thinner and lighter too.

What is measured
Joint rotation angle
Key requirement
Thin profile · hollow bore for cables
Recommended series
IAHInductive Bearingless Rotary EncoderIHHigh-Resolution Inductive Rotary Encoder
02
Service Robot

Robots that move among people

A robot travelling through a restaurant or a logistics center must notice people passing in front of it first, and must know exactly how far its wheels have rolled to find its way back.

What is measured
Nearby people · obstacles, drive-wheel rotation
Key requirement
Safety detection · precise driving
Recommended series
IBArea SensorIHHigh-Resolution Inductive Rotary Encoder
03
Production Line

Counting everything that flows by

Every product passing on the conveyor, and the distance and speed the line has moved. Accurate numbers make accurate production plans.

What is measured
Product quantity · line speed
Key requirement
High-speed counting · on-site display
Recommended series
CTHProgrammable High-Speed Counter TimerIBArea SensorEOptical Rotary Encoder
04
Drone & UAV

The lighter, the longer it flies

Every part carried on a drone is weight traded for flight time. It needs a sensor that reads gimbal and motor rotation in a small body.

What is measured
Gimbal angle · motor rotation
Key requirement
Compact · lightweight · vibration
Recommended series
IHHigh-Resolution Inductive Rotary EncoderMAMagnetic Rotary Encoder
05
Precision Machining

Reading 1/70 the width of a human hair

Machine-tool feed axes and semiconductor equipment stages move in µm steps. The linear encoder that reads linear position determines machining quality.

What is measured
Feed-axis linear position · spindle rotation
Key requirement
µm-level precision
Recommended series
MLMagnetic Linear EncoderEOptical Rotary EncoderECTEncoder Counter
06
EV & Mobility

Every time the wheel turns

The steering angle must be unchanged after power is cycled, and the distance the wheel has travelled must not be off by a hair.

What is measured
Steering angle · travel distance
Key requirement
Position retained after power cycle
Recommended series
MAMagnetic Rotary EncoderEWWheel Type Rotary Encoder
07
Heavy Equipment

Accurate angles, even in the dust

An excavator's boom and arm move all day amid dust, vibration and shock. Non-contact sensing, which reads without touching, lasts longer.

What is measured
Boom · arm · swing angle
Key requirement
Dust · vibration · shock
Recommended series
IAInductive Rotary EncoderMAMagnetic Rotary Encoder
08
Defense

Absolute position that is never lost, even when shaken

Radars and turrets must know exactly which direction they are facing, even under shock and temperature changes.

What is measured
Radar · turret rotation angle
Key requirement
Shock · temperature change · magnetic interference
Recommended series
IAHInductive Bearingless Rotary EncoderIHHigh-Resolution Inductive Rotary Encoder
