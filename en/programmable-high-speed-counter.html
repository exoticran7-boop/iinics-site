Programmable High-Speed Counter Timer

A DIN-rail programmable high-speed counter/timer with x4 encoder multiplication. Counts pulses up to 300 kcps and combines counter, timer, batch counting and prescale in one unit. Plus PC monitoring software: check status, change settings and reset from a single screen to shorten commissioning and maintenance.

Key Features

FEATURE 01
Prescale Function (0.001–999.999)
Converts counts into real length or position.
e.g. a 200 mm roller with a 2,000 ppr encoder: prescale 0.1 in ENC-1 (x1) or 0.025 in ENC-2 (x4) to display and control in mm.
FEATURE 02
Batch Counter for Production Control
Separately counts how many times the preset value or time is reached.
e.g. counting finished boxes while counting 5 apples per box — ideal for lot, packing and production tallies.
FEATURE 03
Wide Input Compatibility
Switch-selectable NPN/PNP open-collector inputs plus a line-driver model (L). Choose 1, 30, 1k or 10k cps to suit the signal (encoder modes run at 300 kcps automatically), six input modes, and external RESET, BATCH RESET and INHIBIT inputs. NPN PNP LINE DRIVER
FEATURE 04
Relay + NPN Outputs & Precise Timing
Two relay contacts (250VAC 2A / 30VDC 2A) and three NPN outputs (OUT1, OUT2, BATCH; 24VDC 30mA). Counter outputs F, N, C, R and timer outputs OND, FLK, INT, OFD; one-shot 0.01–99.99 s, timer ranges from 0.01 s to 999,999 min. RELAY ×2 NPN ×3
FEATURE 05
Setting Protection & Safe Storage
A key-lock switch locks the front keys, and the communication write-protect option (CPrt) blocks preset changes from a PC or PLC. Settings are kept in non-volatile memory, and unsaved edits are discarded after 60 s without key input.
FEATURE 06
Field-Ready Design at a Glance
Dual 6-digit display shows the count (red) and preset (green) together, with status LEDs for presets, outputs and operation. Front keys reset instantly (RST), show the batch value (BAT) and toggle between presets PS1 and PS2 (MODE).

Technology · x4 Encoder Multiplication

ENC Mode · x4 Multiplication

One Encoder Pulse, 4× the Precision

x4 multiplication (ENC-2) counts all four rising and falling edges of the encoder A and B signals. The same encoder delivers four times the resolution, and the phase difference between the two signals automatically determines the direction of rotation.

  • 4× resolution — a 2,000-pulse encoder runs at 8,000 counts per revolution.
  • Automatic direction detection — CW rotation adds (+1), CCW rotation subtracts (−1).
  • 300 kcps high-speed response — handles signal widths down to 0.0017 ms in ENC mode.
  • ENC-1 / ENC-2 modes — choose x1 (1 pulse = 1 count) or x4 (1 pulse = 4 counts). Swap the A and B wires to reverse the counting direction.
