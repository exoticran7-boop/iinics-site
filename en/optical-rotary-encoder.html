Technology · Sensing Principle

LED + Slit Disk + Photo Detector

Interrupted Light Becomes Pulses

Each time LED light passes through the rotating slit disk, the photodetector converts the light ON/OFF into an electrical pulse. The number of slits on the disk is the resolution (P/R) — rotation angle is converted precisely into pulse count.

  • Slit count = resolution — one pulse per slit per revolution (60–3,600 P/R).
  • A/B 90° phase shift — the lead/lag of the two channels determines CW/CCW direction.
  • Z reference pulse — one pulse per revolution marks the home (zero) position.
  • Speed design formula — max. response speed (rpm) = 150 kHz ÷ resolution × 60 (within the 5,000 rpm limit).
