    Hardware and software designed entirely in-house. We own our design data, source code and patents, so improvements and custom development are fast and customer needs go straight into the design.

걸어온 길 · Our Journey

2020년의 첫걸음, 센서 전문기업으로 성장합니다.

From our first step in 2020, growing into a specialist sensor company.
